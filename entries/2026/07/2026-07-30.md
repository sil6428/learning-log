## What I learned

- A detailed model still looks wrong when its parts do not share the same mechanical parent. Grouping the printer carriage and nozzle fixed both the motion and the visual logic.
- Object placement affects camera quality. Moving the rack away from the printer solved clipping more reliably than adjusting the lens alone.
- A CanvasTexture turns a Three.js screen into a readable interface while separate invisible raycast zones keep each desktop file independently selectable.
- Furniture and wall storage make procedural objects feel like parts of one room instead of unrelated models placed on a floor.
- Small construction details, such as a racket shaft, book spines, fan blades, cable paths, pupils, and whiskers, make simple geometry easier to recognize.
- One well-integrated 3D cat fits the site better than a separate 2D character system competing with the room.
- Moving both the focus target and camera along the camera-right vector reserves screen space for the information panel without changing the object's physical position.
- A hidden accessible control list preserves keyboard and screen-reader access even when the visible directory does not fit the intended scene.
- CanvasTexture works well for a stylized operating-system interface because the visual design and the separate 3D hit areas can evolve independently.
- A long animation needs useful state feedback. Percentage, ETA, motion, completion text, and light make the three-minute print readable without forcing the visitor to watch continuously.
- Browser events provide a small bridge between the global terminal and the Three.js room without coupling the two components.
- Closing an information panel and resetting the camera are separate actions. Keeping the current view while restoring controls makes nearby object switching less repetitive.
- A convincing printer needs independent X, Y, and Z motion. Moving the head, bed, and gantry separately makes the mechanism easier to read.
- Revealing prebuilt horizontal slices by height looks more like additive manufacturing than scaling a finished mesh.

## Problem encountered

- The laptop files are nested inside a rotated lid, so their floor markers and camera targets needed world-position conversion instead of their local coordinates.
- The left-wall objects face a different direction from the laptop and rack, so their camera offsets needed to approach from the positive X direction instead of the positive Z direction.
- Removing the 2D cat required cleaning its component, terminal command, Easter-egg documentation, footer house, mobile soundtrack offsets, animation rules, and tests as one feature.
