## What I learned

- A zero-dollar price does not automatically mean an asset has simple reuse terms. The licence must be checked before download and inclusion.
- CC0 assets reduce ambiguity and still benefit from clear creator credits.
- Loading a replacement before hiding its procedural fallback avoids visible gaps on slower networks.
- Bounding-box fitting makes assets from different authors and unit systems easier to compose with one loading helper.
- PBR models need environment light, correct texture handling, and restrained point lights to look better than procedural geometry.
- Higher-quality models have the most impact when the room palette and lighting support them.
- Model quality is not only polygon count. Consistent shading, roughness, color limits, scale, and placement matter more than isolated realism in a stylized scene.
- Replacing imported materials at the loader boundary provides one reusable way to unify assets from different authors without rebuilding their geometry.
