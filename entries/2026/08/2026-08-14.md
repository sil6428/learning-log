## What I learned

- A useful redesign does not need to be discarded when its overall art direction misses the goal. Strong interaction and rendering improvements can be separated from the layout and palette that introduced them.
- Imported models fit a procedural room better when material response is normalized at the loading boundary.
- Hover animation should preserve authored transforms. A visually small interaction bug can quietly undo deliberate scene composition.
- A dark scene still needs strong value separation. Neutral fill and controlled accent lights reveal model form more effectively than increasing every material's brightness.
