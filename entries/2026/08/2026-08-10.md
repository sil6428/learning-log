## What I learned

- Small interaction effects are most useful when they confirm an action instead of continuously competing with the 3D scene.
- A room index improves discoverability while still allowing the scene to remain the primary interface.
- Reference work should identify the specific design idea adopted and clearly state that the implementation is original.
- Motion needs separate behavior for mouse, touch, and reduced-motion users.
- Regression tests should verify both visible interface labels and the source references used in the project.
