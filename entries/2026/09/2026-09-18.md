## What I learned

- A private project can still be explained credibly through a public case study when the implementation boundary, measured tests, and unavailable source are stated plainly.
- Public credibility depends on small details agreeing: collaboration credit, test counts, graduation date, repository visibility, and hosting terminology.
- Stable top-level links are useful for printed QR codes because the underlying resume file can change without reprinting the card.
