## What I learned

- Hardware, memory, storage, operating systems, and networking provide the context needed to understand security telemetry.
- A SOC combines people, procedures, and technology to monitor an environment and coordinate investigations.
- A junior analyst needs to validate alerts, gather relevant context, document findings, and escalate when required.
- Events and alerts are not automatically incidents; triage determines significance, scope, and urgency.
- Documentation and consistent handoffs are part of incident response because multiple analysts or teams may work on the same case.
- These introductory rooms strengthen my defensive-security direction, but deeper hands-on investigation labs are needed before claiming practical SOC proficiency.

## Problem encountered
